DOTS-102 — Diagonal chains and 3-dot minimum mock

Static mock on top of the approved DOTS-101 look. Every situation is drawn below, one under the other. Dashed red rings mark where the player clicks; the white circle marks the pointer or finger; a red dashed line with an ✕ marks a link that is refused. None of these markers are part of the game. The shake is animated in browsers that allow motion; side ghosts show it in the still picture.

AC-1, AC-12 — Diagonal chain growing

  • A dot can be added in any of the 8 directions as long as it is next to the last dot and blue like the starting dot.
  • Diagonal links are drawn center to center, 8 px wide, in the chain’s color — exactly like straight links. They pass between the corner dots without touching them.
  • Keyboard: the focus cursor still moves with the arrow keys (one row or column per press); to add a diagonal dot, move the cursor onto it in two presses and press Enter/Space.

AC-2, AC-3, AC-4 — Ignored clicks

  • 2 away: blue, but two cells diagonally from the last dot — not adjacent.
  • orange: diagonally next to the last dot, but not the chain’s color.
  • in chain: the starting dot is next to the last dot but already in the chain (it is not the previous dot) — ignored. Closed loops stay out of scope.
  • As in DOTS-101, an ignored click gives no feedback.

AC-5 — Crossing link is ignored

  • Only diagonal links can cross; the check is between the new link and any diagonal link already in the chain.
  • No feedback, like any other ignored click (DOTS-101 look: “no feedback on an ignored click”).

AC-6 — 3-dot chain is cleared

  • Score is unchanged from DOTS-101: n×10 + max(0, n−3)×10 → 3 dots = 30, 4 = 50, 5 = 70.
  • Releasing a drag on a 3+ dot chain or pressing Enter/Space on the last dot does the same.

AC-7 — 2-dot chain ended by click: shake, nothing removed

  • Shake only — no “Need 3+ dots” text (D-4).
  • Same result for a tap, or Enter/Space on the last dot.
  • Shake: ~0.3 s, horizontal, about ±5 px, on the dots that were in the chain.
  • With the system setting “reduce motion” on, the dots do not move; instead they show a short static double-outline (like the ghosts in the still picture) for 0.3 s.

AC-8 — Drag released with 2 dots: shake

  • On a touchscreen, lifting the finger after a 2-dot drag does the same.

AC-9 — 1-dot chain ended: silent

    AC-10 — New chain during the shake

    • The board never blocks input for the shake (unlike gravity/refill in DOTS-101).

    AC-11 — Valid move with diagonals, and game over

    • A valid move is 3 same-colored dots that form a connected path of adjacent dots in any of the 8 directions.
    • A pair alone is no longer a valid move, since a 2-dot chain can’t be cleared.
    • A newly dealt board (start, Play again) is re-dealt until it has a valid move under this rule.

    AC-13 — Diagonal drag doesn’t catch the corner dots

    • Dashed square = the cell; dashed circle = the dot circle that counts during a drag (same size as the drawn dot, radius 13 of a 46 px cell).
    • Clicks and taps still use the whole cell (unchanged from DOTS-101).
