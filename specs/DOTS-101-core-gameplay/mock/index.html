DOTS-101 — Core gameplay mock

Static mock: every situation is drawn below, one under the other. Dashed red rings mark where the player clicks and the white circle marks the pointer or finger; neither is part of the game.

AC-1, AC-16 — New game

  • 5-color palette (colorblind-friendly set): blue, orange, green, pink, yellow
  • Header shows the game name “Dots” and the score display.

AC-3, AC-14 — Chain started

  • Highlight: chain dots are drawn larger with a ring in the chain’s color.

AC-4, AC-14 — Chain growing

  • Each added dot is adjacent (no diagonals) to the last dot and has the chain’s color.

AC-5 — Invalid clicks are ignored

  • color: adjacent to the last dot but yellow, not blue.
  • diagonal: blue, but only diagonally next to the last dot.
  • in chain: already in the chain (not the last or previous dot).
  • No visual or sound feedback on an ignored click.

AC-6, AC-14 — Step back

  • Dragging back onto the previous dot does the same.

AC-8 — 1-dot chain ended

    AC-7, AC-10 — Chain cleared

    • Points shown briefly next to the score as +50.
    • Scoring: 2 dots → 20, 3 → 30, 4 → 50, 5 → 70.

    AC-11, AC-12, AC-13 — Gravity and refill

    • Animation takes under about 0.5 s in total.
    • During frames 1–2, clicks, touches and key presses on the board are ignored.

    AC-9, AC-17 — Drag in progress

    • Entering the blue dot under the pointer adds it; releasing ends the chain.
    • Release with 2+ dots clears the chain (as in “Chain cleared”); with 1 dot nothing is removed.

    AC-9, AC-17 — Drag back onto the last dot

    • Re-entering the last dot during a drag does nothing; only releasing ends the chain.
    • The same applies to a finger on a touchscreen.

    AC-18 — Keyboard focus cursor

    • The cursor stops at the board edge.
    • A hint line under the board shows the keys.
    • The cursor and hint are shown only while the board has keyboard focus; during mouse or touch play no cursor is drawn (as in every other situation on this page).

    AC-15 — Game over

    • The board no longer accepts input; the panel sits over the dimmed board.
    • Play again leads to “New game” (score 0, new board).
