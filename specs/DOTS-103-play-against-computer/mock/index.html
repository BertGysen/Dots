DOTS-103 — Play against the computer

One section per situation in the spec. The board rules, chain look, shake, focus cursor and “+points” are unchanged from DOTS-101/102. What is new: the two score cards, the turn indicator, the computer move and the result view.

Opening or reloading the page starts here. There is no mode choice and no solo game.

  • Two score cards: You and Computer, both 0.
  • The card of the side whose turn it is has an outline.
  • The turn indicator under the cards reads Your turn.

Arrow keys move · Enter / Space select

The computer’s 10-dot chain (the longest on this board, found in the AC-9 search order) is being drawn. Here 2 of 10 dots are added.

Timing: 0.4 s pause after the player’s refill, then the dots are added at equal steps over 1 s in total, then the full chain is held for 0.3 s before it is cleared.

Input: clicks, taps, drags and Enter/Space are ignored, and the mouse cursor is the “not allowed” arrow over the board. The arrow keys still move the focus cursor (white square).
